Special session · IJCNN 2027

Trustworthy LLMs & AI Agents for Education

A Special Session on reliable, inclusive, and pedagogically meaningful generative and agentic AI.

Submit your work → Explore the session

01 / Scope & motivation

Building AI that helps people learn — responsibly.

Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI agents offer new opportunities to support students and educators through personalized tutoring, learning material generation, automated assessment, and adaptive feedback. Their educational value, however, depends on more than the ability to generate plausible responses. These systems must provide reliable information, accommodate diverse learning needs, protect sensitive data, and support students’ reasoning and autonomy.

Educational applications introduce challenges that require dedicated methods and evaluation protocols. Hallucinated explanations and misleading feedback can compromise learning, while biases can affect assessment and the support offered to different student populations. Personalization raises questions about privacy and the appropriate use of learner data. Agentic systems introduce further challenges as errors may propagate across planning, retrieval, memory, and tool use.

This session brings together researchers developing and evaluating trustworthy LLMs and AI agents for education. We welcome methodological contributions, benchmarks, and empirical studies addressing reliability, fairness, transparency, privacy, and pedagogical effectiveness.

02 / Topics of interest

Research questions we want to advance.

We welcome contributions across methods, evaluation, and applications — including, but not limited to:

  • 01Fabrication (hallucination) detection, mitigation, and uncertainty calibration
  • 02RAG and knowledge-grounded methods for tutoring and content generation
  • 03Bias and stereotype evaluation in assessment and personalized learning
  • 04Explainability, response verification, and evidence attribution
  • 05Safe planning, memory, tool use, and human oversight in AI agents
  • 06Privacy-preserving methods for learner data and personalization
  • 07Benchmarks for reliability, fairness, and pedagogical effectiveness
  • 08Learner autonomy, trust calibration, and cognitive over-reliance
  • 09Inclusive and equitable AI for students with SEND and disadvantaged backgrounds
  • 10Pedagogically relevant assessment of LLMs and AI agents
